## Journal first pages

`page1-journal.png` (publisher-formatted, fetched 2026-08-10 through Purdue
EZproxy) sits beside the arXiv `page1.png` in 18 of the 19 published papers'
folders. The exception: `2020-laser-noise` (Optics Express) — Optica's site
sits behind a Radware CAPTCHA, which we do not bypass; its arXiv first page
serves meanwhile. The two 2026 arXiv-only preprints have no journal version
yet.

The source publisher PDFs are NOT in this repository (kept in session
scratch only) and must never be committed, per the workspace journal-access
policy. Whether these publisher-formatted page IMAGES may be committed or
published on the site is Jonathan's explicit call to make before go-live.
