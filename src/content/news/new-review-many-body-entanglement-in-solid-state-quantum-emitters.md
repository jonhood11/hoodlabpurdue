Congratulations to Emma Daggett and the entire team! Our review article on many-body entanglement in solid-state quantum emitters has been published in Nature Reviews Materials. Led by Emma Daggett, this comprehensive review covers the path toward achieving many-body entanglement with solid-state quantum emitters integrated in nanophotonic structures. We discuss single-emitter coherence and control, photon-mediated interactions, and the outlook for scalable quantum networks based on these platforms.

  

E Daggett, CM Lange, B Windt, A Danageozian, A Senichev, et al., JD Hood, "Many-body entanglement in solid-state quantum emitters," Nature Reviews Materials, 1-21 (2025).
